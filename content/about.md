👋 Hi! I'm Kitti - a software developer working at the intersection of backend development and machine learning.

My work focuses on building reliable systems, internal tools, and workflows that support data-driven development and experimentation. I’m especially interested in how software and AI can be used together to solve practical problems, streamline operations, and make information more actionable.

I enjoy working in the space between research and implementation, translating ideas into working solutions, prototyping with intent, and continuously improving along the way. I value clean, maintainable code, thoughtful design, and clear documentation as part of building things that last.

## Currently Focused On

- Designing and training ML models for fraud detection and customer profiling using structured transaction data
- Implementing and evaluating deep learning models for offline signature verification
- Building internal tooling and pipelines to streamline experimentation, evaluation, and reproducibility in ML workflows

## Beyond

Outside of work, I like picking up personal side quests, whether it’s revisiting old violin pieces, tweaking home automation setups, or organizing things that probably didn’t need organizing (but now look very satisfying).

Gaming is my comfort zone. I gravitate toward story-rich games and open worlds I can get lost in. If the vibes are right and the soundtrack hits, I’m sold. I play for the plot, the worldbuilding, and those quiet, immersive moments that make you forget the real world for a while.

Maybe that’s why I also love fiction, especially fantasy and romance. I’m drawn to magical worlds, charming characters, and a bit of playful chaos. Give me dragons, witty banter, and adventures that don’t take themselves too seriously, and I’m all in.

Then there are the hyperfixations: cross-stitching, polymer clay, painting tiny things. Random hobbies I dive into just because they seem fun in the moment. It’s chaotic, hands-on learning, and honestly, it keeps life interesting.
